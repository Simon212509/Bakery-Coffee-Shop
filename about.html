About Roll Bakery

Where every bite tells a story.

At Roll, baking is more than just a craft—it’s a passion passed down through generations. Our founders, inspired by the simplicity and tradition of European bakeries, set out to create a space where high-quality bread and pastries could be enjoyed by all.

From the very beginning, we’ve been committed to using the best ingredients and methods. Our sourdough is slow-fermented for up to 48 hours, our pastries are hand-laminated for the perfect layers, and our coffee is sourced from ethical growers who prioritize sustainability.

Our bakery is more than just a place to grab a quick bite—it’s a gathering spot for neighbours, a workspace for freelancers, and a warm retreat from the bustle of daily life.

We also believe in giving back to the community that supports us. That’s why we donate surplus baked goods to local charities and strive to reduce our environmental footprint through responsible sourcing and waste reduction.

At Roll, we invite you to experience the magic of fresh, handmade food. We hope that every visit leaves you feeling nourished, inspired, and eager to return.
